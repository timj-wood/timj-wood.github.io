I did a BSc in Biology at Bristol and stayed on for an MSc in Bioinformatics, starting September 2026. My work so far has been population genetics: detecting introgression and selection in Scottish wildcats for my dissertation. Over the summer, in Bristol's School of Maths, I fitted demographic models to the wildcat–domestic cat joint site frequency spectrum.

My MSc project will move to human genetics, by using neural-network simulation-based inference to model the demography of coeliac disease, funded by a Health Data Research UK scholarship, with Coeliac UK as the charity partner.

You can find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/timj-wood) or check out my [CV](/assets/docs/cv.pdf).
