I completed a BSc in Biology at the [University of Bristol](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/) and stayed on for an MSc in Bioinformatics, which started September 2026. My work so far has been population genetics: detecting introgression and selection in Scottish wildcats for my dissertation. Over the summer, in Bristol's School of Maths, I fitted demographic models to the wildcat–domestic cat joint site frequency spectrum.

My MSc project will move to human genetics, by using neural-network simulation-based inference to model the demography of coeliac disease, funded by a Health Data Research UK scholarship, with Coeliac UK as the charity partner.
