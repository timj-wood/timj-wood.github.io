BSc dissertation. I used pcadapt to scan genome-wide SNP data from 108 cats for regions shaping population structure in the European wildcat, a species threatened by hybridisation with domestic cats. Seven candidate genes with strong outlier signals pointed to GABA transport, taurine transport and calcium signalling, suggesting that behavioural and metabolic pathways may be under selection. PCA showed patterns of introgression, local adaptation and regional divergence, which could inform conservation efforts but would need further testing.
