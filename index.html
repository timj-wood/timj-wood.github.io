Hi! I'm Tim, a Bioinformatics MSc student at the University of Bristol, funded by a scholarship from Health Data Research UK and Coeliac UK. I keep this site as an outlet for personal projects and writing in statistical genetics and machine learning.

For my MSc dissertation, supervised by Prof. Tom Gaunt, I'll be looking for drug-repurposing routes for coeliac disease among drugs already approved for other autoimmune conditions. Before this I did my BSc in Biology at Bristol, working in population genetics with Prof. Mark Beaumont — a dissertation on introgression and selection in Scottish wildcats, and a summer internship in the School of Mathematics fitting demographic models to the wildcat–domestic cat site frequency spectrum.
