Timothy Wood

MSc Bioinformatics, University of Bristol

I'm an HDR UK Scholar studying MSc Bioinformatics at the University of Bristol, where I also did my BSc in Biology. I work on population genomics and statistical genetics: so far, scanning Scottish wildcat genomes for signals of domestic cat introgression and natural selection, and fitting demographic models to their split from domestic cats. For my dissertation, I'll be using neural simulation-based inference to model the genetics of coeliac disease.
