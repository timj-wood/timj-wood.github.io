Hi! I’m Tim, a Bioinformatics MSc student at Bristol interested in statistical genetics and machine learning. Most of what ends up on here will be related to genomics, stats, or topics that I find interesting.

I completed my BSc in Biology at the University of Bristol and stayed on for an MSc in Bioinformatics. My work so far has been in population genetics, under Prof. Mark Beaumont. For my undergraduate dissertation, I built a pipeline to detect introgression and selection in Scottish wildcats. I later completed a summer internship in Bristol’s School of Mathematics, where I fitted demographic models to the wildcat–domestic cat joint site frequency spectrum.

My MSc will move me towards human genetics, funded by a scholarship through Health Data Research UK & Coeliac UK. For my dissertation, supervised by Prof. Tom Gaunt, I plan to create a pipeline which could identify potential drug-repurposing routes for coeliac disease, using drugs already approved for treatable autoimmune conditions.
