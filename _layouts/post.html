--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} Every post opens the same way: a centred title, then one or two centred lines under it. A post in a folder marked `paper: true` in _data/contents.yml (projects/) is laid out like a paper: an optional affiliation above the title, a byline (author, `with:`, `when:`), the `links:`, an optional abstract, and numbered sections once there are three or more. No date or word count. Everything else shows date · words · reading time, then the `links:`. Writing (essays, reviews, stories) also shows its summary as a subtitle, and a review its rating between the subtitle and the date; a post in blog/ shows neither.
